Mech Armor Expanded (MK2 - MK4)
Expands the endgame of Factorio: Space Age by introducing three new, incredibly powerful tiers of Mech Armor. Perfect for dominating planets and surviving the harshest environments.

Features
Mech Armor MK2: Built with Tungsten Carbide. Larger equipment grid (10x16) and enhanced inventory.
Mech Armor MK3: Forged in zero gravity using Cryogenics. Massive grid (10x18) with high resistances.
Mech Armor MK4 (Ultimate): The pinnacle of engineering powered by Promethium. Grants a colossal 10x20 grid and near-total immunity.
Lore & Balance
Crafting these walking tanks requires serious automation. You will need hundreds of Quantum Processors, Supercapacitors, Fission/Fusion reactors, and endgame science packs. To maintain realism, MK3 and MK4 must be assembled in a Cryogenic Plant on a Space Platform with zero gravity conditions.

Расширение Меха-брони (MK2 - MK4)
Мод расширяет эндгейм дополнения Space Age, добавляя 3 новых тира Меха-брони с гигантскими сетками оборудования и практически полным иммунитетом на финальном этапе.

Мод вписан в логистику игры: ультимативные костюмы требуют сборки в криогенных заводах в невесомости, использования термоядерных реакторов и прометия из глубокого космоса.
