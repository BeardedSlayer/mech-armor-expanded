Date: 2026-10-27
  Major Features:
    - Removed hard dependency on Power Armor MK3 and Space Age. The mod now works standalone with the base game as well as with Space Age.

    - Added full compatibility with Krastorio 2 and Krastorio 2 Spaced Out (K2SO).

  Changes:
    - Rebalanced Space Age technology prerequisites and crafting ingredients for MK2, MK3, and MK4 to strictly follow planetary progression (Fulgora -> Aquilo -> Promethium).

Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-07-29
  Features:
    - Dynamic Damage Compatibility: The mod now automatically detects custom damage types from other mods (e.g., cold and frost from custom biter mods) and dynamically assigns appropriate resistances to MK3 and MK4 armors on startup.
    - Smart Mod Integration (O Shattered Planet): Added seamless integration without hard dependencies. If the mod is detected, the MK4 recipe automatically switches to require prometheum plate and updates the technology prerequisites accordingly.
    - Lore-Accurate Assembly: Added strict environmental conditions for crafting. MK2 now requires the intense electromagnetic atmosphere of Fulgora, and MK4 assembly is strictly locked to zero-gravity environments.
  Balancing:
    - Complete Grid Rebalance (Legendary+ UI Fix): Redesigned all equipment grids to vertical rectangles. This mathematically ensures that even at Legendary+ quality, the massive UI window will not overflow standard screens at 100% UI scale. Base grids are now: MK2 (10x16), MK3 (10x18), and MK4 (10x20).
    - Hardcore Resistance Overhaul: Scaled back MK4 absolute immunities from 100% to a maximum of 95% for realistic wear-and-tear. The armor now acts as the ultimate physical hull that absorbs extreme shock only after energy shields collapse.
  Bugfixes:
    - Kinetic Impact Vulnerability: Added impact resistance scaling across all tiers (up to 95% on MK4) to prevent instant one-shots from massive kinetic forces (like Territorial Demolishers) bypassing energy shields.
